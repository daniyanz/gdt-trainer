Why these sources

The main GD&T standards, ASME Y14.5 and ISO 1101, are paid documents and are not publicly available for free download. This site therefore relies on free public sources and paraphrases their ideas instead of quoting the standards.

NASA bases its dimensioning and tolerancing rules on ASME Y14.5. NASA KSC-GP-435 Volume I invokes ASME Y14.5-2018, and NASA GSFC-X-673-64-1F invokes ANSI Y14.5M-1982. The NIST and NASA slide decks listed below also teach ASME Y14.5.

Privacy

  • No accounts and no personal data. Your lesson and challenge progress is saved only in this browser, using localStorage, and is never sent to a server or database. The Reset button on the Progress page deletes it.
  • When you press "Explain my mistake (AI)", the site sends only the question and the answer you chose to OpenAI, through this site's server. No name, email or other personal information is sent.
  • To keep costs down, the server counts AI requests per IP address for the current day. This count is kept in memory only and is not stored.
